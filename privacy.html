Privacy Policy

Son of God is a service-management app used by church service teams to manage people, servants, attendance and visits (Aftkad).

What we collect: when you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. You may add your photo, phone number, birthday, area and a Google Maps link. Service admins add the people they serve.

How it is used: only to run the app for your church service. Data is visible only to members of the same service, based on their role (Admin, Edit or View). We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Storage: data is stored securely on Supabase. You can ask your service admin, or contact us, to remove your data at any time.
